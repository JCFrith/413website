The Pipeline

We Own the Process From Intake to Output

No handoffs to subcontractors, no interpretation gaps between teams, no variability across geographies.

1

Portfolio Ingestion

Client provides a location list. We pre-populate survey templates via CSV, configure any custom attributes, and build the execution schedule around your decision window.

  • Location data imported and validated
  • Survey templates configured with standard and custom fields
  • Deployment schedule aligned to renewal cycle, audit, or operational deadline
2

Field Execution

Internally trained field technicians deploy on burst schedules, capturing every data point on site using a mobile collection platform with offline sync, GPS stamping, and photo verification at point of capture.

  • Standardized capture logic enforced by the platform, not left to inspector judgment
  • Thermal imaging with documented ambient conditions
  • Every attribute tied to inspector, timestamp, and visit ID
  • Offline-capable, no connectivity dependency in the field
3

Quality Control

Completed surveys are reviewed for completeness, consistency, and data integrity before delivery. Incomplete or ambiguous captures are flagged and resolved, not passed through.

  • 100% completion verification on every required attribute
  • Photo documentation reviewed for clarity and relevance
  • Exception flagging for locations outside client-defined tolerances
4

Data Delivery

Verified data delivered in structured formats ready for your existing workflows. No reformatting, no manual cleanup, no interpretation layer between the field and your team.

  • Structured data exports (CSV, Excel) for RMIS and internal databases
  • Per-site PDF reports with full photo documentation and traceability
  • Raw dataset access for broker submissions and carrier requirements
Field Teams

Our Field Teams

We hire, train, and deploy our own field technicians. No gig-economy networks. No subcontractor variability. Every technician is trained to one controlled methodology before they enter the field.

Internally Trained

Every technician is trained on the full survey protocol, equipment operation (including thermal cameras), and data capture standards before deployment. Training is conducted by 413 Solution, not outsourced.

Single Methodology

The capture logic is built into the collection platform. Technicians execute the same standardized workflow at every location, eliminating interpretation variance across inspectors and geographies.

Burst Deployment

Field teams deploy on concentrated schedules optimized for portfolio coverage. A fly-in/out execution model allows rapid nationwide deployment without maintaining permanent regional offices. Pilots of 100 to 500 locations typically complete in 1 to 3 months. Programs of 1,000+ locations typically complete in 4 to 6 months.

Chain of Evidence

Every data point is traceable to a specific technician, visit, timestamp, and GPS coordinate. The same evidence integrity standard used in law enforcement documentation, applied to commercial property data.

  • No quality degradation in secondary or rural markets
