Report Contents

What's Inside Every Report

Each per-site survey covers the full spectrum of property risk data points. Every field is standardized across the portfolio and supported by photo documentation.

COPE: Construction

  • Total square footage
  • Wall construction and ISO class
  • Roof construction and deck type
  • Floor construction
  • Stories above and below ground
  • Photo verification per attribute

COPE: Occupancy & Protection

  • Occupancy type and classification
  • Sprinkler systems and coverage
  • Fire alarms and detection
  • Fire extinguisher status
  • Hood suppression systems
  • Public protection and ISO class

NatCat Exposure

  • FEMA Risk Index scores
  • Earthquake spectral acceleration
  • Flood zone and DFIRM data
  • Hail zone and expected size
  • Wind: distance to coast and tier
  • Wildfire, tornado, tsunami, volcanic

AIR Modifiers

  • AIR construction and occupancy codes
  • General secondary modifiers
  • Earthquake secondary modifiers
  • Hurricane secondary modifiers
  • Wind and earthquake modifiers
  • Photo documentation per modifier

Slip-Trip-Fall & Workers' Comp

  • Indoor walkway and entrance conditions
  • Parking lot and outdoor surfaces
  • Lighting, drainage, and ADA compliance
  • Kitchen and back-of-house hazards
  • Equipment guards and PPE access
  • Storage and ergonomic observations

Thermal & Equipment

  • Main panel thermal scan
  • Sub-panel thermal scans
  • Temperature differential documentation
  • Equipment serial numbers
  • HVAC testing dates
