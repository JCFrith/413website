Positioning

What We Are, and What We Are Not

413 Solution occupies a specific, intentional lane.

Raw Data Only

We collect and deliver verified field data: COPE attributes, exposure documentation, site condition verification. We do not analyze it, score it, or layer interpretation on top of it. Your analysts, brokers, and underwriters do that. We make sure what they work with is accurate, standardized, and traceable.

No Fragmented Networks

We hire, train, and deploy our own field technicians. No gig-economy networks. No subcontractor variability. Every technician is trained to one controlled methodology before they enter the field. One standard, everywhere.

Designed for Scale

We work with portfolios of 1,000+ locations. Team structure, data capture, quality control, and delivery workflows are all built for that scope without sacrificing consistency at any individual site.

In the Field

How We Operate

Every technician deploys with the same equipment, the same platform, and the same protocol, regardless of location or portfolio.

Mobile Collection Platform

Standardized survey templates pre-populated via CSV, with offline sync capability, GPS stamping, and photo verification at point of capture. The capture logic is built into the platform, not left to inspector judgment.

Thermal Imaging

Professional-grade thermal camera used at every survey to document insulation gaps, moisture indicators, electrical anomalies, and HVAC performance. Ambient conditions recorded with calibrated instrumentation.

Burst Deployment Model

Field teams deploy on concentrated fly-in/out schedules optimized for portfolio coverage. No permanent regional offices. Rapid nationwide deployment scaled to the engagement.

Evidence-Grade Documentation

Every data point tied to a specific technician, visit, timestamp, and GPS coordinate. The same chain-of-evidence discipline used in law enforcement, applied to commercial property data.
