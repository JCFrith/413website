Thermal Layer

Electrical Panel Thermal Scan

Where thermal is in scope, technicians scan the main electrical panel and sub-panels with the dead front in place. The scan shows breakers running hotter than the rest of the panel, a condition a visual inspection cannot see.

Readings are surface temperatures of breaker bodies and handles. Conductors and lugs behind the dead front are not visible to the camera, and every report says so. The single highest temperature difference at each location is recorded with its NETA MTS priority classification.

Evidence

Photo Verification at Every Point of Capture

Every data point is supported by high-resolution, timestamped photography with GPS coordinates embedded at point of capture. Photos are tied to the specific attribute, inspector, and visit, creating a complete visual chain of evidence for every location in the portfolio.
